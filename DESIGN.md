## Brand & Style

This design system embodies maternal tenderness, human dignity, global solidarity, and unconditional support for mothers and children worldwide. It translates the visual narrative of the association's identity—hands offering refuge, maternal bonds, and flourishing childhoods—into a cohesive digital sanctuary.

### Emotional Target & Aesthetic Movement
The user experience evokes comfort, trust, heartfelt generosity, and solemn empowerment. The aesthetic sits at the intersection of **Gentle Humanitarian Minimalism** and **Warm Tactile Editorial**. It eschews sterile NGO templates in favor of generous breathing room, graceful serif typography paired with warm geometric sans-serifs, tender pink gradients, and deeply respectful deep-ink typography that provides institutional gravitas.

## Colors

The color palette directly draws from the central logo artwork: maternal fuchsia, tender petal pink, luminous cream backgrounds, and deep obsidian charcoal for high readability.

### Color Tokens & Roles
- **Primary (`#D94676`)**: Active fuchsia representing vital energy, solidarity, and immediate engagement (calls to action, donation highlights, focused outlines).
- **Secondary (`#F48FB1`)**: Soft, nurturing pink for decorative accents, subtle badges, hover overlays, and supportive iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#E0A868`)**: Subtle warm gold/rose-gold accent honoring humanitarian milestones, VIP/monthly donor highlights, and trust seals.
- **Neutral Deep (`#1E1A1C`)**: Deep black-plum charcoal for text hierarchy, establishing institutional gravitas without harsh coldness.
- **Surface & Backgrounds**:
  - `surface-canvas`: `#FFFDFD` (pure warm white base)
  - `surface-subtle`: `#FFF4F8` (delicate rose mist for card backdrops and alternating sections)
  - `surface-card`: `#FFFFFF` with whisper-soft warm pink borders (`#FCE7F0`)

## Typography

The typography pairs a stately, high-contrast serif (`Bodoni Moda`) for titles and organizational impact with a clear, humane geometric sans-serif (`Montserrat`) for long-form reading, UI controls, and uppercase institutional labeling.

- **Bodoni Moda**: Evokes timeless dignity, care, and editorial prestige, echoing the central typographic hallmark of the emblem.
- **Montserrat**: Provides exceptional legibility, balanced geometry, and an approachable rhythm for administrative information, donation tiers, and field reports.
- **Label Uppercase**: Employs generous tracking (`0.18em`) mimicking the statutory inscription `"ASSOCIATION"` and guiding thematic anchors.

## Layout & Spacing

A 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored within a maximum width of `1200px` ensures optimal reading comfort and content balance. 

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `Mobile`: `< 640px` (4-column grid, `16px` gutters, fluid content stacking)
  - `Tablet`: `640px – 1024px` (8-column grid, `24px` gutters)
  - `Desktop`: `> 1024px` (12-column grid, `32px` gutters, centered alignment)
- **Rhythm**: Spacing enforces generous top-to-bottom separation (`space-3xl` between story sections) to maintain an unhurried, reassuring pace suitable for humanitarian engagement.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft ambient light and tinted feather shadows instead of harsh drop-shadows, creating a comforting, protective aura.

- **Surface Layering**: Cards and raised surfaces rely on faint rose-tinted shadows: `0px 8px 30px rgba(217, 70, 118, 0.07)` and `0px 2px 8px rgba(30, 26, 28, 0.04)`.
- **Card Framing**: Elevated surfaces feature a gentle 1px border colored `#FCE7F0` that preserves silhouette clarity against warm cream backdrops.
- **Modals & Overlays**: Light frosted glass overlays (`rgba(255, 253, 253, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) maintain emotional connection with background imagery during donation or sign-up flows.

## Shapes

Shapes prioritize soft, protective curves reflecting the nurturing hands and spherical embrace depicted in the organization's logo.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem / 8px`)**: Used for input fields, interactive micro-elements, and compact badges.
- **Large Radius (`1rem / 16px`)**: Standard for content cards, impact metric highlights, and image containers.
- **Pill / Fully Curved (`9999px`)**: Reserved for action buttons (Donation CTA, Contact), tag filters, and featured badges to emphasize kindness and touchability.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Action / Don Solidaire)**: Pill-shaped, gradient or solid fill `#D94676` with crisp white Montserrat medium text. Hover state shifts to `#C23662` with a subtle elevation boost (`rgba(217, 70, 118, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Sensibilisation / Découvrir)**: Pill-shaped, transparent interior with a 1.5px solid border in `#D94676` and text in `#D94676`. Hover fills with `#FFF4F8`.
- **Tertiary (Discrète)**: Underlined text in `#1E1A1C` with a soft arrow glyph and rose transition.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Form Controls**: Crisp white background, `8px` rounded corners, `1px` border in `#E8CCD8`. On focus, transitions to a `2px` border in `#D94676` with a delicate glow (`rgba(217, 70, 118, 0.15)`).
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes**: Heart-friendly soft geometry with `#D94676` checked fill, optimized for monthly donation frequency selection (e.g., "Don Ponctuel" vs. "Don Mensuel").

### Cards & Humanitarian Modules
- **Cause & Story Cards**: Rounded `16px` containers, white canvas with `#FCE7F0` border. Imagery sits under a light warm gradient mask to integrate naturally with typography.
- **Donation Selector Chips**: Pill buttons with `#FFF4F8` default surface and `#D94676` active fill with bold white price tags.
- **Key Metrics / Impact Stats**: Centered numerals set in `Bodoni Moda` (`#D94676`) paired with uppercase category subtitles in `#1E1A1C`.
